What We Do

Every Roof, Start to Finish

Replacements, leak repairs and roof check-ups for Augusta homes, explained before we start.

Residential · Roof Replacement

Old Roof Off, Nu Roof On

When a roof is past repairing, it comes off down to the deck and goes back on from the ground up: underlayment, shingles, flashing and ridge, with the yard cleaned up after.

  • Underlayment and shingles
  • Flashing, vents and ridge
  • Clean-up when the job is done

Residential · Leak Repair

Leaks Fixed at the Source

Not every roof needs replacing. Lifted shingles, worn flashing and leaks around walls and windows can often be repaired, and we tell you plainly which one your roof needs.

  • Leaks traced to the source
  • Flashing resealed
  • Damaged shingles replaced

Residential · Roof Inspection

Know Before You Spend

Not sure what your roof needs? We walk it, check the shingles, flashing and vents, and give you an estimate so you can decide between a repair and a new roof.

  • Shingles, flashing and vents checked
  • Repair or replace, explained
  • Estimate before any work
